# What is an Algorithm
>Finite well defined steps to solve a problem.
>what sequence of steps should i follow to solve this problem.

 ex:-Imagine your college has 1,000,000 student records.
 and we have to find out a candidate with roll no 231500067.

---
# Algorithim Thinking

- what exactly is the problem. (defining the problem)
- what information do I have ? (inputs & Constraints)
- what do I need to produce ? (outputs)
- what is the simplest solution ?(Brute Force)
- How much work does it do ?(TC)
- Can I  make the algo faster.(Optimization)

# Important Concept: Constraints
| Constraint      | Often consider              |
| --------------- | --------------------------- |
| `n ≤ 10`        | Almost anything             |
| `n ≤ 20`        | Sometimes `2^n`             |
| `n ≤ 100`       | `O(n³)` may be possible     |
| `n ≤ 1,000`     | `O(n²)` often possible      |
| `n ≤ 100,000`   | Usually `O(n)`              |
| `n ≤ 1,000,000` | Usually close to `O(log n)` |
| n<=10m          | O(log n)                    |
